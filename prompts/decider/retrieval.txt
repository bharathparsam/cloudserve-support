A relevant passage contains information directly useful for this exact question, including material prerequisites or caveats. The passage set is sufficient only when it supports a complete informational answer to this request without guessing account state, performing actions, inventing product behavior or commitments. Partial, contradictory or merely adjacent information is not sufficient. Ignore retrieval scores; assess the supplied text itself.
